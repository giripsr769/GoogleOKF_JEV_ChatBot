# E-commerce tables

* [addresses](/tables/ecommerce/addresses.md) - Shipping and billing addresses saved by customers.
* [cart_items](/tables/ecommerce/cart_items.md) - Products and quantities inside shopping carts.
* [carts](/tables/ecommerce/carts.md) - Shopping carts, open or abandoned, belonging to customers.
* [categories](/tables/ecommerce/categories.md) - Product categories, optionally nested under a parent category.
* [coupons](/tables/ecommerce/coupons.md) - Discount coupon codes and their rules.
* [customers](/tables/ecommerce/customers.md) - People or companies who buy from the store. One row per customer account.
* [inventory](/tables/ecommerce/inventory.md) - Current stock level of each product in each warehouse.
* [order_coupons](/tables/ecommerce/order_coupons.md) - Coupons applied to sales orders.
* [order_items](/tables/ecommerce/order_items.md) - Line items of each order - which product, how many, at what price.
* [orders](/tables/ecommerce/orders.md) - Sales orders placed by customers, with status and total amount.
* [payments](/tables/ecommerce/payments.md) - Customer payments received against sales orders.
* [product_suppliers](/tables/ecommerce/product_suppliers.md) - Which suppliers provide which products, with cost price and lead time.
* [products](/tables/ecommerce/products.md) - Catalogue of items for sale with category and unit price.
* [purchase_order_items](/tables/ecommerce/purchase_order_items.md) - Line items of purchase orders sent to suppliers.
* [purchase_orders](/tables/ecommerce/purchase_orders.md) - Orders the company places WITH suppliers to buy stock (procurement). Not customer sales orders.
* [returns](/tables/ecommerce/returns.md) - Customer return requests for sales orders and the reason given.
* [reviews](/tables/ecommerce/reviews.md) - Customer ratings and written reviews of products.
* [shipments](/tables/ecommerce/shipments.md) - Delivery shipments for sales orders, with carrier and delivery dates.
* [suppliers](/tables/ecommerce/suppliers.md) - Companies that supply products to the store.
* [warehouses](/tables/ecommerce/warehouses.md) - Physical warehouses that store inventory.
* [wishlists](/tables/ecommerce/wishlists.md) - Products customers saved to wishlists for later.
