# Tables

# E-commerce

* [addresses](/tables/ecommerce/addresses.md) - Shipping and billing addresses saved by customers.
* [cart_items](/tables/ecommerce/cart_items.md) - Products and quantities inside shopping carts.
* [carts](/tables/ecommerce/carts.md) - Shopping carts, open or abandoned, belonging to customers.
* [categories](/tables/ecommerce/categories.md) - Product categories, optionally nested under a parent category.
* [coupons](/tables/ecommerce/coupons.md) - Discount coupon codes and their rules.
* [customers](/tables/ecommerce/customers.md) - People or companies who buy from the store. One row per customer account.
* [inventory](/tables/ecommerce/inventory.md) - Current stock level of each product in each warehouse.
* [order_coupons](/tables/ecommerce/order_coupons.md) - Coupons applied to sales orders.
* [order_items](/tables/ecommerce/order_items.md) - Line items of each order - which product, how many, at what price.
* [orders](/tables/ecommerce/orders.md) - Sales orders placed by customers, with status and total amount.
* [payments](/tables/ecommerce/payments.md) - Customer payments received against sales orders.
* [product_suppliers](/tables/ecommerce/product_suppliers.md) - Which suppliers provide which products, with cost price and lead time.
* [products](/tables/ecommerce/products.md) - Catalogue of items for sale with category and unit price.
* [purchase_order_items](/tables/ecommerce/purchase_order_items.md) - Line items of purchase orders sent to suppliers.
* [purchase_orders](/tables/ecommerce/purchase_orders.md) - Orders the company places WITH suppliers to buy stock (procurement). Not customer sales orders.
* [returns](/tables/ecommerce/returns.md) - Customer return requests for sales orders and the reason given.
* [reviews](/tables/ecommerce/reviews.md) - Customer ratings and written reviews of products.
* [shipments](/tables/ecommerce/shipments.md) - Delivery shipments for sales orders, with carrier and delivery dates.
* [suppliers](/tables/ecommerce/suppliers.md) - Companies that supply products to the store.
* [warehouses](/tables/ecommerce/warehouses.md) - Physical warehouses that store inventory.
* [wishlists](/tables/ecommerce/wishlists.md) - Products customers saved to wishlists for later.

# Human resources

* [attendance](/tables/hr/attendance.md) - Daily attendance records for employees.
* [departments](/tables/hr/departments.md) - Company departments such as Sales, Engineering and HR.
* [employees](/tables/hr/employees.md) - Company staff members. Internal people, not store customers.
* [job_titles](/tables/hr/job_titles.md) - Job titles and their pay grade bands.
* [leave_requests](/tables/hr/leave_requests.md) - Employee leave and time-off requests.
* [performance_reviews](/tables/hr/performance_reviews.md) - Periodic performance reviews and ratings of employees.
* [salaries](/tables/hr/salaries.md) - Employee salary records. SENSITIVE.
* [training_courses](/tables/hr/training_courses.md) - Internal training courses employees can take.
* [training_enrollments](/tables/hr/training_enrollments.md) - Which employees enrolled in which training courses and their completion.

# Finance

* [accounts](/tables/finance/accounts.md) - Chart of accounts used by the general ledger.
* [bank_transactions](/tables/finance/bank_transactions.md) - Bank statement lines: deposits and withdrawals.
* [budgets](/tables/finance/budgets.md) - Planned spend per department per fiscal year.
* [expenses](/tables/finance/expenses.md) - Employee expense claims such as travel and meals.
* [invoice_items](/tables/finance/invoice_items.md) - Line items on customer invoices.
* [invoices](/tables/finance/invoices.md) - Invoices billed to customers for sales orders (billing documents with due dates).
* [ledger_entries](/tables/finance/ledger_entries.md) - General ledger postings: debits and credits against accounts.
* [tax_rates](/tables/finance/tax_rates.md) - Sales tax rates by country.
* [vendor_bills](/tables/finance/vendor_bills.md) - Bills received from vendors that the company must pay (money OUT).
* [vendors](/tables/finance/vendors.md) - External companies the business pays for services and goods (accounts payable).

# Customer support

* [chat_sessions](/tables/support/chat_sessions.md) - Live chat sessions between customers and agents.
* [customer_feedback](/tables/support/customer_feedback.md) - Post-support satisfaction (CSAT) scores from customers.
* [escalations](/tables/support/escalations.md) - Tickets escalated to a higher tier or manager.
* [knowledge_articles](/tables/support/knowledge_articles.md) - Help-centre articles agents and customers can read.
* [sla_policies](/tables/support/sla_policies.md) - Service level targets for response and resolution by priority.
* [support_agents](/tables/support/support_agents.md) - Customer support agents.
* [support_teams](/tables/support/support_teams.md) - Support teams that agents belong to.
* [ticket_categories](/tables/support/ticket_categories.md) - Categories used to classify support tickets.
* [ticket_comments](/tables/support/ticket_comments.md) - Conversation messages on support tickets.
* [tickets](/tables/support/tickets.md) - Customer support tickets raised about problems or questions.
